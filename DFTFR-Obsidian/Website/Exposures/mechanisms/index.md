 A collection of devices, gears, and apparatuses gathered over recent months — some inherited, some found, some sent along by correspondents who seemed glad to be rid of them. I have photographed each as faithfully as I am able, in the hope that a careful visual record might succeed where my own understanding of their mechanism has, in every case, eventually failed.
